Translation Quality System

AI speed. Human responsibility.

GT-CnT combines the efficiency of modern AI translation technology with professional human review, structured as a multi-stage quality process.

01

AI Draft

An appropriate AI model is selected according to the characteristics of the document, producing a high-efficiency initial translation.

02

Expert Review

A professional translator reviews terminology, context, proper nouns, tone and meaning.

03

Native Check

Native-level review checks whether the content is natural and appropriate within the target language and business culture.

04

Final QA

GT-CnT performs the final review before delivery.

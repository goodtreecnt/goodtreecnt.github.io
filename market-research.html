SAMPLE DELIVERABLE — MARKET SNAPSHOT ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE
MARKET
Korean skin-booster products in Japan (illustrative category)
MARKET STRUCTURE
Fragmented: imported products sold mainly through clinic-direct and distributor channels, not general retail.
KEY COMPETITORS
Mix of domestic Japanese brands and imports from Korea and Europe; 3–5 names typically dominate clinic shelf space.
DISTRIBUTION CHANNELS
Clinic-direct sales, regional medical distributors, and trade-show sourcing.
ENTRY CONSIDERATIONS
Import registration requirements and clinic purchasing cycles noted as the main timeline factors.
OPPORTUNITY NOTE
Regional distributors outside Tokyo identified as comparatively underserved.

Cosmetics & Labeling

Japan is the world's third-largest cosmetics market — and one of the strictest to enter. A single mislabeled ingredient can mean a customs rejection or a recall. GT-CnT's reviewers work specifically against Japan's Pharmaceutical and Medical Device Act (PMD Act) and MHLW labeling standards.

Why It Matters

Why cosmetics translation needs a specialist.

Cosmetics labeling sits at the intersection of language, chemistry and Japanese regulation — general translation is not enough.
